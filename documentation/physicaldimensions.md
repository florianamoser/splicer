Splicer is modular and designed for transport. Below are the key dimensions and considerations for use and transport.

### In Use

- When fully set up, Splicer occupies a footprint of approximately **2 × 3 meters**.
- A working clearance of at least **1 meter** around the machine is required; **2 meters** is recommended for optimal operation and maintenance access.
- The total setup weight is approximately **235 kg**.

### In Transport

- Splicer can be transported within the footprint of a single Euro pallet (120 × 80 cm).
- Once off the pallet, its two main components (Camera Tower and Sample Mover) are mounted on wheels and can be maneuvered through standard interior doors (**minimum 80 cm width**).
- **Transport weight:** to be determined.
