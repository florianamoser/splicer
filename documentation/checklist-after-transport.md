### Tools required
- Allen key (size 5)
- Allen key (size 2.5)
- Bubble level

### Checklist

- [ ] Place the camera tower at its final location.
- [ ] Lower the leveling feet of the camera tower.
- [ ] Check the level of the camera tower using the bubble level.
- [ ] Mount the XY platform to the camera tower using 2 × M6 screws at the base.
- [ ] Level the XY platform with the bubble level.
- [ ] Attach the overhead extrusion.
- [ ] Release the camera transport locking strap.
- [ ] Mount the screens to the operator area, starting with the lower screen, followed by the left and right screens.
- [ ] Attach power and signal cables to the screens.
- [ ] Attach the tool post to the rotating C axis.
- [ ] Attach the servo to the RGB filter changer.
- [ ] Connect data cables to the X, Y, and C axes.
- [ ] Attach the 48 V power supply to the X, Y, and C axes.
- [ ] Reconnect the cable for Streaming Camera 2.
- [ ] Reconnect the cable for Streaming Camera 3.
- [ ] Mount and connect Streaming Cameras 1–4.
- [ ] Mount the lens to the Splicer camera.
- [ ] Verify all cable connections.
- [ ] Plug in the 220 V power supply.
- [ ] Check homing of the XY axes at initial startup.
