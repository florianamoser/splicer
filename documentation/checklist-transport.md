### Tools required
- Hex key (size 5)
- Hex key (size 2.5)
- Bubble level

### Checklist

- [ ] Switch off all systems.
- [ ] Switch off main power switch.
- [ ] Unplug in the 220 V power supply.
- [ ] Remove Splicer camera lens and wrap in bubble wrap.
- [ ] Attach Splicer camera lens cover.
- [ ] Disconnect and remove streaming Cameras 1–4.
- [ ] Disconnect 48 V power supply to the X, Y, and C axes.
- [ ] Disconnect data cables to the X, Y, and C axes.
- [ ] Remove the servo to the RGB filter changer.
- [ ] Remove the overhead extrusion.
- [ ] Remove the tool post to the rotating C axis.
- [ ] Fold down table. Store Keyboard in Rako box.
- [ ] Disconnect power and signal cables to the screens.
- [ ] Remove the screens to the operator area, starting with the left and right screens, followed by the lower screen.
- [ ] Wrap screens in bubble wrap and store in Rako box.
- [ ] Manually move focus gantry all the way to the back.  
- [ ] Attach the camera transport locking strap to shift gantry.
- [ ] Detach the XY platform to the camera tower unscrewing 2 × M6 screws at the base.
- [ ] Raise leveling feet of the XY platform.
- [ ] Raise leveling feet of the camera tower.
- [ ] Tilt XY Platform vertical on transport mount and secure with ratchet straps.
- [ ] Store misc. parts, tools, streaming cameras, lens in Rako box.
