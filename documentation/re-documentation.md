This is a fictional email exchange that stages the internal frictions and cross-disciplinary misunderstandings encountered during the development of Splicer. To craft a surreal and somewhat ironic narrative, I have chosen to present each position in the first person and sign all of them with my name. The intention is not to ridicule the disciplines involved, but to highlight the clash between differing perspectives and priorities, ultimately serving as a way to communicate the realities of splicer's development process.
